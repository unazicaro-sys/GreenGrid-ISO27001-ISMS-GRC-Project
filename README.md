# GreenGrid – ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS & GRC Project

**Role:** Compliance & Statement of Applicability (SoA) Lead  
**Framework:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
**Focus:** Risk Management, Compliance, Control Mapping and Audit Readiness

## Project Overview

GreenGrid is a renewable energy organisation operating across West Africa, managing solar and wind installations, SCADA/OT infrastructure, energy distribution interfaces and commercial billing operations.

This project focused on developing an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) with particular attention to information security risks, control applicability and compliance documentation.

## My Role and Responsibilities

As the **Compliance & SoA Lead**, I was responsible for ensuring alignment between the Information Security Risk Register and the Statement of Applicability.

Key contributions:

- Reviewed and aligned identified risks with risk treatment decisions.
- Mapped relevant ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls to identified risks.
- Documented applicability, implementation status and justification for all 93 Annex A controls.
- Ensured consistency between the Risk Register and SoA.
- Supported compliance documentation and audit readiness through clear control traceability.

## Key Deliverables

1. Information Security Risk Register.
2. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Statement of Applicability (93 Annex A controls).
3. Control applicability and implementation assessment.
4. Risk treatment and control mapping.
5. ISMS compliance and audit-readiness documentation.

## Key GRC Principle

The Risk Register identifies and evaluates information security risks and informs risk treatment decisions.

The Statement of Applicability documents the necessary controls, their applicability, implementation status and justification for inclusion or exclusion.

**The objective:** Ensure that identified risks, selected controls and documented justifications remain consistent and traceable.
